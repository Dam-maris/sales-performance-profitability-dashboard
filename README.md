# Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 1,000 sales transactions using Microsoft Excel to explore sales performance and profitability across time, customer types, regions, and products.

The analysis uses PivotTables and visualizations to identify sales trends, compare customer and regional performance, and highlight the products generating the highest profits.

## Key Areas Analyzed

* **Sales Trends** — Analysis of sales performance over time.
* **Customer Segmentation** - Comparison of sales between Business and Individual customers.
* **Regional Performance** - Analysis of sales across different regions.
* **Product Profitability** - Identification of the top five products by profit.

## Key Findings

* Sales show seasonal variation, with peak sales occurring in July ($248K) and August ($262K).
* Sales are distributed between Business and Individual customers.
* Sales performance varies across regions.
* The analysis identifies the top five products by profit.
